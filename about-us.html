Who We Are

About Us

Results, Experience, and Integrity

At SeekSave Investments, LLC, we combine decades of investment experience, and personalized, values-driven service to help clients grow and protect their wealth.

We provide investment advisory services designed to optimize performance, generate higher returns, and help ensure that investment assets—401(k), IRA, pensions, and brokerage accounts—last longer in retirement.

We manage comprehensive, multi-account portfolios using strategies grounded in proven financial principles and enhanced by cutting-edge AI technology.

Our approach is built on:

  • ✔ Risk tolerance, diversification, and asset allocation
  • ✔ Long-term perspective, not market timing
  • ✔ Customized plans based on personal goals

Process: Discover → Plan → Invest → Monitor

Meet Our Team

Meet Our Team

Peter Oluseyi Obitade, Ph.D., MBA

Founder & Chief Investment Officer

SeekSave Investments, LLC is founded and led by Dr. Peter Obitade, who holds dual Ph.D. degrees in Finance and Information Technology, along with an MBA.

With over 20 years of investment experience—spanning equity trading, wealth planning, and fintech innovation—Peter has successfully built a multi-million-dollar personal portfolio and helped numerous clients work toward financial independence.

He has held senior management positions at three Fortune 500 companies—IBM, Chevron, and Wells Fargo—where he led strategic initiatives and developed AI-powered solutions for portfolio optimization and risk management.

Peter is driven by a passion for integrity, lifelong learning, and faith-based financial stewardship. He specializes in helping fellow retirees and pre-retirees maximize their financial future through personalized, value-centered guidance.

Outside of his advisory work, Peter enjoys mentoring students, studying market history, and volunteering at his church, reflecting his commitment to both professional excellence and community service.

Dorcas Obitade, MBA

Market Research & Financial Analyst

Dorcas holds an MBA with a focus on financial strategy and business analytics.

She specializes in equity market research, economic trend analysis, and financial data visualization to support client portfolio decisions and macroeconomic forecasting.

Known for her attention to detail and passion for client education, Dorcas brings both analytical rigor and a warm, client-focused approach to the team.

Outside of work, she enjoys reading, cooking, and spending time with family.
