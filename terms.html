Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 07/01/2025  |  Last Updated: 07/04/2025

SeekSave Investments, LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy of our clients. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information obtained in the course of providing investment advisory and wealth management services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect nonpublic personal information about you, including:

  • • Name, address, phone number, email
  • • Social Security number, tax ID number
  • • Financial account numbers and balances
  • • Investment objectives and risk tolerance
  • • Employment and income details
  • • Tax, legal, and estate planning data
  • • Transaction history and financial statements

2. How We Collect Your Information

We may collect information:

  • • Directly from you via applications, forms, consultations, or interviews
  • • From custodians, account aggregators, or third-party financial institutions
  • • Through secure online portals or email communications
  • • From other professionals at your request (e.g., CPAs, attorneys)

3. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used to:

  • • Provide customized financial and investment advice
  • • Manage and monitor your investment portfolios
  • • Prepare financial plans and retirement projections
  • • Assist with tax, estate, or insurance strategies
  • • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share your information:

  • • With third-party service providers (e.g., custodians, software vendors) as needed to service your account
  • • With your authorized professionals (CPA, estate attorney)
  • • As required by law or regulatory authorities (SEC, FINRA, IRS)

All service providers and vendors are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Safeguarding Your Information

We maintain physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your information:

  • • Data encryption and password protection
  • • Secure file sharing and client portals
  • • Role-based access control within our systems
  • • Regular security audits and compliance reviews

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • • You may request a copy of the personal information we maintain about you.
  • • You may correct or update your information at any time.
  • • You may limit sharing of certain data by notifying us in writing.

7. Former Clients

We maintain the same level of protection for your personal information even after your advisory relationship with us has ended.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. The most recent version will always be posted on our website or provided upon request.
