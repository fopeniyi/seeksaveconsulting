Three rules we do not break.

Own what we understand

We hold 15 to 25 companies. If we cannot explain how a business makes money in two sentences, we do not buy it.

Keep costs visible

One flat annual fee, shown in dollars on every statement. We earn nothing from the products we select.

Think in decades

Turnover is low by design. Most holdings stay for seven years or more, so taxes and trading costs stay low too.
